01 / Search

Coded message

This unfinished search keeps the rotor order (I–II–III), reflector (B), rings (AAA), and plugboard (empty) fixed. It tries 16,900 starting positions and favors decoded text with lower single-letter entropy. A few dozen letters can give a false winner; try a paragraph of at least 100 letters for a more useful guess. Even then, treat the result as a clue, not a reliable decode.
