Coded message

This unfinished search tries all 17,576 starting positions for the selected rotor order, or 60 rotor arrangements when you choose to search all orders. Set the known reflector, rings, and plugboard values below. It favors decoded text with lower single-letter entropy. A few dozen letters can give a false winner; try a paragraph of at least 100 letters for a more useful guess. Even then, treat the result as a clue, not a reliable decode.
